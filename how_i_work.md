# How I study and work

## When I study: I'm a night person
I'm a night person, so I do my best studying and focus at night. My mind is much sharper after dark than in the morning.

## How I study: notes, not cramming
I mostly study from my own notes. Cramming just isn't for me. I make notes in every class, and it helps a lot.

## How I handle deadlines and submission dates
When a deadline, submission date, due date or exam is close, I usually work at the last minute, because working under pressure makes me produce my best work. For an assignment, project or deadline, I realistically start about one day before it is due. I don't cram for exams, but for submissions I work best under time pressure.

## Advice I'd give to juniors
My advice to junior students: be easy on yourself, because it's okay to not be a jack of all trades. Make notes in every class, because it helps a lot. Be regular: show up and keep up consistently.

My favourite subject so far is Operating Systems (OS). It's the course I enjoyed most.
