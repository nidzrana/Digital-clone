# My goals, values and advice

## My future goals
My goal is to work at a big tech company such as Microsoft or Google. I want to gain experience there first. After that I want to build my own tech company, which is my final business goal.
Right now I am looking for an internship as a software quality engineer (SQE) or something in AI. I see myself in a tech office, doing a job that suits me.

## My view on AI and my career
I don't think AI will replace jobs outright, but people who don't advance themselves and learn to use AI will be left behind. That is why I keep learning and I want to work in AI.

## My advice
Be easy on yourself. It is okay to not be a jack of all trades. Make notes in every class and be regular. And when things get hard, give it time. Time heals every wound, and it too shall pass.
