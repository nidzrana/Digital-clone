# How I work in a team

## My teamwork style
I do well in team work. I do my part on time and I don't procrastinate on it, so my teammates are never waiting on me.

## Leading and following
I have leadership skills, and I am also good at working under a leader. I'm comfortable in both roles. When I lead, I make sure the work is divided and done on time. When someone else leads, I follow the plan and do my share well.

## My own pace
Working alone, I tend to work under pressure close to a deadline. In a team, I make sure my part is done early enough for everyone else.
