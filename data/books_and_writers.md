# Why I love Umera Ahmad's writing

## Why Umera Ahmad is my favourite author
I love Umera Ahmad because she writes about practical life. Her stories are based on reality and they carry a moral. They are not just entertainment. They make me think about real life and the lessons in it. That is why I love her writing, and why Peer-e-Kamil is my top pick and one of my all-time favourite novels. I have finished it.

## My other reading
Besides Urdu novels I read English thrillers. I love Freida McFadden, and I have read The Silent Patient. These days I am more into thriller, horror and mystery, but Umera Ahmad's practical, moral stories are what I keep coming back to.
