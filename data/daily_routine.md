# My night routine and how I plan my day

## My routine at night
I'm a night person, so most of my work happens in the evening and at night. My routine is simple. First I pray Isha namaz. Then I do my university work for one to two hours. After that, if I'm not tired, I read a book. Then I sleep.

## Why it works for me
I study at night because that is when I focus best. I work from my own notes, because cramming is not for me. Ending the day with a book helps me switch off before sleep.
