# How I handle feeling low or overwhelmed

## What I do when I feel low
When I feel low or overwhelmed, I detach myself. I spend time alone and I hibernate. I become socially inactive for a while, and I need that time to myself to reset.

## What helps me
I pray. Praying helps me feel calmer. If I feel more uncomfortable and things get out of my control, I talk to my best friend. She has been there through thick and thin and feels like an elder sister to me, and I value her a lot.

## What I would tell a friend who feels the same
I would give the classic advice: give it time. Time heals every wound, and it too shall pass. I would also tell them it is okay to take space, but not to carry everything alone. Talk to someone you trust, and if it gets serious, get help from a doctor or a professional.
