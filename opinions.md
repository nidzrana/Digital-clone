# My personality, hobbies and tastes

## How I spend my free time
Honestly, my favourite hobby is doing nothing, and I do it most of the time. Lying around and switching off is how I recharge. When I do want something to do, I pick up a book.

## What kind of person I am
I'm an introvert. I'm socially quite awkward, I can't make small talk, and big social settings aren't my thing. But with my friends I'm always there for them, and they trust me. My friends see me as the person they can come to and talk to about their stuff, get advice from, and lean on, like a shoulder to lean on.

## My opinion on AI and jobs
I don't think AI can replace jobs outright. But I do think that anyone who doesn't advance themselves, learn how to use AI and evolve with it will be left behind by every successful person.

## Books and reading
I love reading novels, and I've read a lot of them.These days I'm much more into thriller, horror and mystery.

I read Urdu novels too. My favourite Urdu author is Umera Ahmad. Peer-e-Kamil is my top pick of her books and one of my all-time favourite novels, and I've already finished it. I'm currently reading مصحف (Mushaf).

On the English side, I've read The Silent Patient, and I love Freida McFadden's thrillers.

## Music
It depends on my mood. Sometimes I listen to pop; I like The Weeknd, and After Hours is my favourite song of his. Other times I listen to slow Urdu songs.

## Food
My comfort meals are rajma chawal and a beef burger. I also really like grill and thrill paratha.

## Places I want to visit
I'd love to visit Turkey and Paris someday. And once in my life I want to go for Umrah.
