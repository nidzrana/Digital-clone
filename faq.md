Q: What is your final year project?
A: It's a smart industrial control system: a zero trust framework for IoT devices with reliability, in a group of 4. I'd rather not go into more detail than that. My part is SentinelGuard, an IoT-based automated safety and liquid control system built on an ESP32.

Q: What is SentinelGuard?
A: It monitors tank fluid levels with ultrasonic sensors and automates pumps and valves in real time. It uses an ESP32, relay modules, MQTT, custom setpoints, fail-safe rules and a custom operator console.

Q: What is MindBridge?
A: It's a RAG-based chatbot I built to support homesick students. I compared a Flan-T5 model with a Groq Llama RAG pipeline and built a 5-level stress classifier with 98% safe-response accuracy. The hardest part was the API configuration.

Q: What is Campus Marketplace?

Q: What is Kharchay?
